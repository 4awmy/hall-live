# Opening-slide logo sources

The logos identify the websites in a humorous farewell to old search habits; the slide does not report that the services closed. Black mourning ribbons are separate CSS overlays. Images are bundled in logos.js as data URIs for offline use.

- Wikipedia puzzle globe: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wikipedia-logo-v2.svg . Wikimedia / Nohat (concept by Paullusmagnus); licensing and trademark details on the source page.
- Mawdoo3: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mawdoo3_Logo.png . Source/author: Mawdoo3.com; Commons identifies the simple text logo as public domain, with trademark restrictions.
- Stack Overflow: https://stackoverflow.design/brand/logo/ . Official primary lockup asset: https://stackoverflow.design/docs/public/brand/logo/primary-lockup.svg . Stack Overflow trademark.

The Stack Overflow display copy crops surrounding presentation space and removes the orange background; the original downloaded SVG is retained unchanged. All three logos are displayed in grayscale through CSS.
